Storage

Where is everything stored

Your board lives in a folder on your computer. Nothing important is kept in the browser.

photos/ – the original photos, videos, stickers and PDF documents (and your wallpaper photo), copied in unchanged
thumbs/ – smaller copies of your photos so the board stays fast (safe to delete; they’re made again when needed)
board.json – the layout: title, sections, positions, captions, sizes, notes, to-do lists, calendars, index cards, pins and yarn, templates, the shelf, labels, links, your sticker collection, fonts, photo filters, sticker finishes, theme, card style and sound settings

A web page can’t open your file explorer or see the full folder path, because browsers keep that private. The button below opens the system folder dialog inside your board’s folder, so you can see where it is. Press Cancel to leave everything as it is. If you pick a different folder, the board switches to it.
